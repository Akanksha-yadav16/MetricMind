# Metric Definitions

## 1. Total Revenue
Description:
Sum of the revenue generated from all orders.

Formula:
SUM(order_total)

---

## 2. Total Orders
Description:
Total number of orders placed.

Formula:
COUNT(order_id)

---

## 3. Total Customers
Description:
Number of unique customers.

Formula:
COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id)

---

## 4. Average Order Value
Description:
Average amount spent per order.

Formula:
Total Revenue / Total Orders

---

## 5. Top Selling Product
Description:
Product with the highest quantity sold.

Formula:
SUM(quantity) GROUP BY product

---

## 6. Top Customer
Description:
Customer who generated the highest revenue.

Formula:
SUM(order_total) GROUP BY customer

---

## 7. Revenue by Product
Description:
Revenue generated by each product.

Formula:
SUM(order_total) GROUP BY product

---

## 8. Orders by Customer
Description:
Number of orders placed by each customer.

Formula:
COUNT(order_id) GROUP BY customer
