# Metric Definitions

These definitions describe the metrics currently calculated by `semantic_layer/metrics.py`.

## 1. Total Revenue

Revenue generated from order items after discount.

```text
SUM((UNIT_PRICE * QUANTITY) - DISCOUNT)
```

Source table:

```text
ORDER_ITEMS
```

---

## 2. Total Orders

Total number of rows in the `ORDERS` table.

```text
COUNT(*)
```

Source table:

```text
ORDERS
```

---

## 3. Total Customers

Total number of rows in the `CUSTOMERS` table.

```text
COUNT(*)
```

Source table:

```text
CUSTOMERS
```

---

## 4. Average Order Value

Average revenue per distinct order.

```text
Total Revenue / COUNT(DISTINCT ORDER_ID)
```

Source table:

```text
ORDER_ITEMS
```

---

## 5. Top-Selling Product

Product with the highest total quantity sold.

```text
SUM(QUANTITY)
GROUP BY PRODUCT_NAME
ORDER BY SUM(QUANTITY) DESC
LIMIT 1
```

Source tables:

```text
ORDER_ITEMS
PRODUCTS
```

---

## 6. Top Customer

Customer with the highest total order-item revenue after discount.

```text
SUM((UNIT_PRICE * QUANTITY) - DISCOUNT)
GROUP BY CUSTOMER
ORDER BY SUM(...) DESC
LIMIT 1
```

Source tables:

```text
ORDER_ITEMS
ORDERS
CUSTOMERS
```

---

## 7. Revenue by Product

Revenue generated by each product after discount.

```text
SUM((UNIT_PRICE * QUANTITY) - DISCOUNT)
GROUP BY PRODUCT_NAME
```

Source tables:

```text
ORDER_ITEMS
PRODUCTS
```

---

## 8. Quantity Sold by Product

Total units sold for each product.

```text
SUM(QUANTITY)
GROUP BY PRODUCT_NAME
```

Source tables:

```text
ORDER_ITEMS
PRODUCTS
```

---

## Important Implementation Note

The current MetricMind backend uses the curated `CUSTOMERS`, `ORDERS`, `PRODUCTS`, and `ORDER_ITEMS` tables in the `ANALYTICS` schema.

The raw Olist CSV files in `data/raw/` are included for dataset exploration/reference and are not directly queried by the current semantic layer.
